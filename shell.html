These stars are not advice, and they are not a forecast. They count how many measurable signals currently line up — past price behaviour, published analyst views, reported company financials, and for Bitcoin and Ethereum the money that has already moved into or out of their ETFs, plus how busy Bitcoin's chain is. Every star can be traced to its arithmetic: open Show the working on any row. Momentum is also the mechanism by which people buy tops, so the risk band is a hard cap, never an average.
This is not a list of what to buy. Once a day a robot scores a wide set of assets with the exact same arithmetic as your own watchlist — nothing here is chosen because it looks like it will go up. Something appears because its measurable evidence changed enough to be worth a glance, not because the app thinks it is a good idea.
